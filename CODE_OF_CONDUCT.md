# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kudu to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:
- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Helping newcomers find their way.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [Manas Dutta](https://github.com/manasdutta04),
through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/manasdutta04/kudu/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting them on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
